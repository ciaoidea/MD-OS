## Contributors

Contributors retain copyright in the contributions they create unless a
separate written agreement says otherwise. MD-OS does not require contributors
to assign their copyright to Alessandro Rizzo or to the project.

Accepted contributions must follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the
[Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](DeveloperCertificateOfOrigin.txt).
Authorship and contribution paths are recorded through Git commit metadata,
`Signed-off-by`, and any applicable `Co-developed-by`, `Reviewed-by`, or
`Tested-by` trailers.

The collective phrase “Alessandro Rizzo and MD-OS contributors” is not a claim
that the founder owns contributions written by other copyright holders.

Use [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff) for the preferred project-level citation.
